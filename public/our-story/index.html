A dream on Main Street

Photo: storefront on Main Street

Live-n-Comfort opened in December 2019 in the Village of Jeffersonville, right on Route 108 at the foot of Smugglers’ Notch. [Add one or two sentences on what led Barbara to open the shop and where the name came from.] Seven years later, it is still the shop of my life’s dream.

Curated with intention

Nothing lands on our shelves by accident. I look for pieces with a story: handmade ceramics, small batch teas, bags and totes sewn by real people, beautiful stationery, organic linens and seasonal treasures you won’t find anywhere else. Many come from artisans and makers I know personally, and supporting them matters to me as much as the gifts themselves.

You’ll find a wide range of price points, too, so there’s always something for everyone on your list, and something for you.

Part of the Comm-Unity

This little shop exists because of the people who walk through the door. Neighbors, visitors passing through on their way to the Notch, families picking out a birthday gift, and friends who stop in just to visit Annie, my corgi. We host pop ups with local makers and change the shop with every season, so there’s always something new to discover.

Thank you kindly for supporting our sweet Shop.
Barbara
